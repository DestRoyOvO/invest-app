# Factor research

Generated by `npm run research` (scripts/research-factors.ts) from the settings in scripts/evaluation.config.json and the bar cache of `npm run evaluate`. Re-running on the same bars reproduces this file, lib/factor-research.json and lib/risk-calibration.json exactly.

## Summary

- **No factor passes the adoption rule.** Across 21 pre-registered tests (7 factors x 3 horizons, signals 2016-01-04 to 2026-10-28, 78 large US stocks) the smallest p-value is 0.063 (Low beta, 5 sessions, IC -0.027), and its Holm-adjusted p is 1.00.
- Low volatility and low beta run against their literature direction on raw returns, but net of beta times SPY's return their ICs turn small and positive: the raw pattern is market exposure in a decade when SPY returned 14.6% a year, not stock selection.
- The universe is today's leaders picked with hindsight: equal-weighted it returned 19.1% a year against 12.0% for RSP and 14.6% for SPY.
- With about 78 names the smallest IC this design can detect with 80% power is 0.033 at 5, 0.066 at 21, 0.107 at 63 sessions, above typical large-cap factor ICs (about 0.01 to 0.03): the universe cannot validate a factor either way.
- Risk, unlike return, is forecastable: trailing 63-session volatility ranks next quarter's volatility with a mean rank correlation of 0.72 (never below 0.43), trailing beta ranks next quarter's beta at 0.77, and the volatility forecast's 10-session bands held 71.8% and 94.5% of moves (68.3% and 95.4% for normal returns).

## Design (fixed before the results)

- Universe: the 78 stocks of lib/universe.ts with history; calendar: SPY's sessions. Signals every session from 2016-01-04; a date counts with at least 20 stocks.
- Factors at the close of session k, from data up to k only (lib/factors.ts), signed so that higher values are expected to do better:
  - 12-1 momentum
  - Short-term reversal (minus the 21-session return)
  - 52-week high (close over the 252-session high)
  - Low volatility (minus the 63-session volatility)
  - Low beta (minus the 252-session beta to SPY)
  - Technical score (model v4)
  - Composite (equal-weight z-scores of the five style factors)
- Forward return over h sessions: adjusted open of k + 1 to adjusted close of k + h, minus the same-day equal-weight mean of all stocks (excess).
- Statistic: mean cross-sectional Spearman IC on non-overlapping dates (every h sessions from the first signal), t-test across dates, Holm and Benjamini-Hochberg adjustment over all 21 tests. All-dates ICs with a Newey-West t (lag h) are a robustness check.
- Rule: A factor is called predictive only if its Holm-adjusted p over the 21 pre-registered tests is below 0.05 and its mean IC has the same sign in every subperiod.
- Subperiods: 2016-01-01 to 2020-12-31, 2021-01-01 to 2023-12-31, 2024-01-01 to 2026-12-31 (by signal date).

## Power

Minimum detectable mean IC (two-sided 5% test, 80% power) from the median standard deviation of the per-date IC across the seven factors:

| Horizon | Dates | IC standard deviation | Minimum detectable IC |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 5 | 545 | 0.274 | 0.033 |
| 21 | 129 | 0.265 | 0.066 |
| 63 | 43 | 0.246 | 0.107 |

Published large-cap factor ICs are about 0.01 to 0.03 per month, so a null here means "cannot tell", not "disproven". The per-date IC varies mostly with time rather than with sampling noise (about 0.11 for 78 names), so more years help more than more names.

## Risk: what the data does forecast

Window 2016-01-01 to 2026-11-06. The forecast (lib/risk-forecast.ts) is fixed in advance, not tuned: daily volatility = sqrt(0.5 x var20 + 0.5 x var252) of daily log returns, the simplest mix of short and long memory (HAR models, Corsi 2009).

- Persistence over the next 63 sessions (43 non-overlapping dates): trailing 63-session volatility vs realized volatility, mean rank correlation 0.721 (min 0.427, t 53.6); trailing 252-session beta vs realized beta 0.765 (min 0.349, t 39.3).
- 10-session bands over 210230 stock-days: inside plus/minus 1 sigma 71.8%, inside 2 sigma 94.5% (20-session volatility alone: 67.3% and 91.7%). Tails are fatter than normal, so the 2-sigma band is approximate.
  - 2016 to 2020: 73.0% / 94.3% (96943 stock-days)
  - 2021 to 2023: 72.0% / 95.3% (58219 stock-days)
  - 2024 to 2026: 69.3% / 93.9% (55068 stock-days)
- QLIKE loss (lower is better) over 210230 stock-days: blend 0.426 against 0.549 for the 20-session estimate.

## Limitations

- Hindsight universe: 78 of today's largest stocks, chosen in 2026. Delisted and shrunken companies are missing, which flatters returns and favours stocks that fell and recovered. A point-in-time universe (historical index membership) is the fix and is not available from Yahoo.
- Power: with this few names and years, realistic factor ICs are below what the tests can detect. A null result here does not refute the published evidence.
- Regime: one decade, mostly a strong US bull market. Low-risk and reversal effects are known to depend on the market's volatility.
- Data: Yahoo daily charts, dividend and split adjusted; returns before costs; no fundamentals (they have no point-in-time history here). Yahoo recomputes adjusted closes in single precision on every request, so a fresh download can move the last digits; the cached bars reproduce every number exactly.
- Intended use: lib/factor-research.json backs descriptive factor percentiles shown with their measured IC, never a buy or sell signal; lib/risk-calibration.json backs the risk ranges with their measured coverage.
